Password management is crucial for maintaining online security. I religiously use [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/) as my password manager and source of truth for authentication. That said, I still memorize three essential passwords:

1. The master password to my Bitwarden vault
2. The local user password for logging into my personal computers
3. The password to my email account, commonly used for password resets

With this approach, I don't need to memorize any other passwords, simplifying my digital life while maintaining strong security practices.
