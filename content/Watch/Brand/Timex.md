Founded in 1854 in Waterbury, Connecticut as the Waterbury Clock Company. Thomas Olsen purchased the Waterbury Clock Company in New York in 1941 and renamed it Timex, a portmanteau of the names of _Time_ magazine and _Kleenex_. In 1944, the company became insolvent but was reformed into Timex Corporation. In 2008, the company was acquired by Timex Group B.V. and was renamed Timex Group USA.

Famous slogan: "Takes a Licking and keeps on Ticking"
